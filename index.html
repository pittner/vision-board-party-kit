The 2-hour agenda

  1. 0:00 Arrive, drinks, everyone writes 2027 in one word on a sticky note and keeps it hidden.
  2. 0:15 Prompt round. Each guest draws two prompt cards and answers them out loud in one minute. Nobody comments.
  3. 0:35 Hunt. Cut pictures and words that match your answers. Rule: no picture without a reason you can say in one sentence.
  4. 1:05 Build. Glue the board. The one word goes in the middle, the first step goes in the bottom corner.
  5. 1:40 Share. Two minutes each: the word, one picture, the first step and its date.
  6. 1:55 Phones out. Photograph the board or scan the QR on the card sheet and save your goal as a lock screen.

Why the prompt round comes first: guests who answer two questions before touching a magazine cut pictures for a reason, and the board ends up being about them, not about the magazines you happened to have.

Questions

How do you host a vision board party?

Invite 4 to 12 people, give everyone a board and magazines, and run it in four parts: two prompt cards answered out loud, 30 minutes of cutting, 35 minutes of building, and a short round where each guest names one picture and one first step. The printable prompt cards and the 2-hour agenda on this page cover the whole evening.

What do you need for a vision board party?

A board per guest, old magazines, scissors, glue sticks, sticky notes, markers and the printed prompt cards. Guests without magazines can make their board on their phone with the free maker linked from the QR code on the card sheet.

How long should a vision board party last?

About two hours. Shorter and nobody finishes the board, longer and the sharing round drags.

When is the best time to host a 2027 vision board party?

Between mid-November and mid-January. A party before New Year lets every guest take the first step before 1 January.

Can I host a virtual vision board party?

Yes. Send the invitation with a video link, share the prompt cards on screen, and have guests build on their phones with the free maker. Each guest ends with a 1080x1920 lock-screen wallpaper instead of a paper board.
